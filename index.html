        Tips that work

        • Start with the hardest task while your energy is highest.
        • Put your phone in another room during focus sessions.
        • Test yourself instead of re-reading. Recall beats review.
        • Study the same subject at different times, not in one long block.
        • Sleep before an exam. It does more for memory than a late cram.

        About Student Digital Hub

        Our mission: give every student the tools to study well, without cost or complexity.

        What it is

        Student Digital Hub is a free toolkit that brings a timer, planner, calculators, notes and a curated list of learning resources into one simple website.

        Why it was created

        Studying often means switching between scattered apps and tabs. We built one calm place for the basics so you can spend your energy on learning.

        Who it is for

        High-school and college students, and anyone studying for an exam, a certificate or just for fun.

        How your data is handled

        Everything you save lives in your browser's local storage on this device. There are no accounts and nothing is sent to a server.
